## Why
The maintainer (INBOX 2026-09-28, DECISION 1): **BF1 Naboo Plains always has the Gungan fambaa and its shield**, in every mode, as
the map's unique feature. The shield is a dome that units and vehicles walk through but bolts cannot cross, generated by a
fambaa and covering part of the map. The Instant Action NAB1 the port bakes cannot show it.

  The regular NAB1 world names them only in the `perma_spawn10` pad props. Not in the IA PCSX2 pairs.

- 2026-10-07, from VTAT's RE (`analysis/re-turret-ai.md` 4-5, ab64c9c; verifier-SUPPORTED): **what drives the fambaa's
  turret is not an AI.** Both classes' TURRET1 author `PilotType self`, `Controllable 0`, `AutoFire 1`,
  `MaxTurnSpeed 0`, `MaxPitchSpeed 0`. In BF1 a self-piloted seat never gets a controller (C14-C17); the turret's own
  tick `FUN_00223850` does two things for these flags: with `Controllable = 0` and the weapon's LockOnRange > 0 it
  casts a ray of that length along the turret's fixed aim every tick and makes the entity it hits the weapon's
  target (0 on a miss; B1:137839-137866), and with `AutoFire = 1` it steps that weapon's trigger byte as held while
  the owner's alive bit is set (B1:138079-138118). From the disc: the GENERATOR's weapon
  `nab_weap_bldg_fambaa_generator` is a `repair`-class weapon with LockOnRange 50 and `YawLimits 150 210` (it works
  on whatever stands within 50 m behind it); the SHIELD's `nab_weap_bldg_fambaa_shield` is a `shield`-class weapon
  with LockOnRange 0 (no ray; AutoFire alone keeps it on; keys 0x5a9201b6 "60.0 30.0" and 0xddb924a1
  "fambaa_shield" not named). `Controllable 0` also keeps the seat out of the list bots are offered seats from.
  **Open, for this card's RE:** what links the turret's tick for a seat nobody sits in; whether the two weapon
  classes fire on the trigger's level or its press edge (AutoFire's byte never passes through 1); what the `shield`
  and `repair` weapon classes do when "fired". Reader's notes: `engine/artifacts/lead_tmp/vtat/AGENT_BF1.md` A.

## Build spec (this card; the look is FAMBV, the post role FAMBP)
1. Bake (`bake/src/map.rs`, a pull like `side_classes.rs`): bf1_nab1 also takes NAB1_H.LVL's two entc + two wpnc,
   model `nab1_walk_fambaa` (+ LOWD), skel, collision prim `p_collision12`, clips `fambaa_idle` / `fambaa_death`
   (bank `nab_walk_fambaa`), textures; the `fambaa_shield` fx config into effects.ron.
2. Spawn (`game/src/vehicle_pads.rs`): in every NAB1 mode the two pads spawn their `ClassHisDef` class for port
   team 1 (PS2 team 2 = Republic / Alliance, CP1's owner) once at battle start; the generator REPLACES perma_spawn13's
   Republic / Alliance kaadu (the historical layout). Default, reversible (STATUS Calls).
3. The building (`core/src/sim/vehicles.rs` or a new `core/src/fambaa.rs`): immobile, MaxHealth 10000, damage x the
   ordnance's BuildingScale, death plays `fambaa_death` then removes it; never respawns; no post role (FAMBP).
4. The shield (new `core/src/shield.rs` + the bolt / grenade sweeps, `core/src/projectile.rs`, `core/src/sim/combat.rs`,
   `core/src/sim/ordnance.rs`): ellipsoid half-axes 60 / 30 / 60 at the shield fambaa's origin; ONLY ordnance tests
   it and ONLY an inward crossing from outside counts; a bolt that meets it dies there (its impact) and takes
   Damage x ShieldScale from the 100000 points; grenades bounce off its outside; blasts and every non-ordnance query
   ignore it. Points drain 100000 /s while on; on whenever points > 0; each shot of the shield weapon resets them to
   100000; the weapon holds 2 clips and fires as soon as one is loaded; the generator (alive, its fixed ray 50 m from
   its hp_fire at yaw 180 hitting the shield fambaa) adds 1 clip every 0.1 s. Off = no body, no effect.
