Historical technical record. Recorded state before archival was `review`. Current scope and ownership are on [issue #86](https://github.com/sprucey149/openbf/issues/86).

## Why
`analysis/difficulty-proposal.md` §5 (Q5): measure "hard enough" without the maintainer playing. A bot that the
human-only layers treat as a human, with a fixed human profile, on both sides of each map, per tier, against
target win rates. Then calibrate its "good" profile on the maintainer's own matches (card DBASE).

## Change
- `core::ai::proxy` (OURS, a measuring tool): `Unit::proxy_human: Option<Profile>` (hashed only when set, kept
  through respawns), `Unit::is_human()`. As a TARGET / team member a proxy is a human: the pressure weight
  (`perception::choose_target_with`), TOKN tokens (`ai::tokn`), BF2's whole aim error against a human
  (`gunner_foot::error_vector`, `Sim::aim_blocks` every tick), the D12 human-damage multiplier and the bots'
  friendly-fire immunity (`resolve_bolt`, `blast_unit`), the bleed handicap's "team with a human"
  (`apply_team_handicap`), and `proxy::humans_on(sim, team)` - **card DTEAM's hook** (human count per team,
  proxies included). As a SHOOTER and for cheats it stays a bot (`is_player` false: no cheat reaches it, no damage
  buff). Its profile replaces the tier's per-unit columns (`Sim::unit_tier`, `Sim::skill_of`): aim skill (no Elite
  cap), reaction ticks, REACT chances, grenade escape, view, and `circles` (fights on the move).
- Strengths, PROVISIONAL until DBASE: **good** skill 20, reaction 6 ticks, react 35/25/30 (stay 10 %), grenade 0.95,
  view x1.5, circles; **average** skill 17, reaction 9, react 30/15/20, grenade 0.60, view x1.25, own style.
- `proxy::TARGETS` (the maintainer's, good proxy, both sides averaged): Easy >= 90 %, Normal 77-90 %, Hard 45-60 %,
  Elite 25-40 %; `proxy::passes`.
- `round_to_outcome`: `SWEEP_PROXY=<team>:<average|good>[,skill=..,reaction=..,view=..,circles=..,strafe=..,roll=..,
  move=..,grenade=..]`, `SWEEP_TEAMS`, `SWEEP_NOFILL`, `SWEEP_ERA=cw`, `SWEEP_MISSION_DIR`; a stderr `round` line per
  round (winner, margin, the proxy's kills / deaths / shots / hits / accuracy / life); `--difficulty-check
  [--strength] [--seeds N] [--tiers ..] [maps]` (default maps bf1_yav2 tat1 bf1_nab2, 16 v 16) printing PASS/FAIL per
  tier; `BLACKBOX_ON=end`; `MATCHLOG_DIR` (the proxy's match log). stdout unchanged.
- `arena`: `ARENA_PROXY=<team>:<strength>`, a stderr `proxy` line.
- `game::matchlog`: the windowed game's whole-round log of the player (dev mode, `OPENBF_MATCHLOG=0` off):
  `<blackbox dir>/matches/<YYYYmmdd-HHMMSS>_<map>.jsonl` at the outcome or on quit. Rows: shots, hits dealt/taken
  (direct/blast), kills, deaths, spawns (class), samples every 5 ticks (position, yaw, stance, speed across/along,
  stick, seat), sightings; footer: result, tickets, every cheat on at any tick. Rows preallocated (60k).
- Black box schema 10: the `session` block (the same tally, whole battle) and a digest line.
- `scripts/human_profile.py show|compare|calibrate`: the profile from match logs (refuses a log with cheats);
  `calibrate` plays the human's map / tier / team for each grid point and ranks the points.

## Evidence (this branch, main's baked data, missions regenerated by `gen_missions.py --ps2-only`)
Difficulty check, good proxy, 3 maps x 2 sides x 8 seeds = 48 rounds per tier, 16 v 16:

| tier | proxy team wins | target | margin | proxy K/D | acc | life s | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 28/48 (58 %) | >= 90 % | +16.2 | 2.22 | 0.264 | 64 | FAIL |
| Normal | 23/48 (48 %) | 65-80 % | -0.4 | 1.48 | 0.240 | 57 | FAIL |
| Hard | 24/48 (50 %) | 45-60 % | -0.2 | 0.69 | 0.221 | 38 | PASS |
| Elite | 25/48 (52 %) | 25-40 % | +2.2 | 0.53 | 0.195 | 35 | FAIL |

Average proxy: Easy 65 %, Normal 50 %, Hard 54 %, Elite 44 % (K/D 1.72 / 1.02 / 0.49 / 0.36). Reading: with both
teams at one tier the round stays a coin flip at every tier (one proxy in 16 does not carry it); the tier moves only
the proxy's own K/D. Hard's PASS is the coin flip landing in its band. The proposal's asymmetric rows (DTEAM) are
the lever the targets need.

GCW balance (bots only, Normal, 16 v 16, 8 seeds, original and factions swapped): the most even are **bf1_yav2**
(Yavin 4: Arena; 5/8 and 5/8, margins +5 / +6), **tat1** (Tatooine: Dune Sea; 5/8, 3/8, +10 / -7), **bf1_nab2**
(Naboo: Theed; 3/8, 5/8, -14 / +11). Lopsided by side: bf2_kam1 (team 0 16/16), bf2_myg1, bf2_uta1, bf2_dea1
(team 1 16/16), bf1_rhn1, bf1_hot1 (15/16), bf2_pol1, bf1_rhn2, bf1_end1. Lopsided by faction: bf1_kas2 (Rebels
+73), bf2_yav1 (+40), bf1_bes1 (Empire +37), bf2_tat3 (+35), bf1_nab1 (+34). Map bias is left as it is.

## Calibration recipe (DBASE)
1. Build from `engine/`: `cargo build --release -p game --example round_to_outcome` and play with
   `cargo run --release -p game` (dev mode on by default: the match log is on).
2. Instant Action: YAVIN 4: ARENA (BF1), Galactic Civil War, Conquest, Normal, no cheats; play 2-3 whole matches to
   the end screen. Files: `engine/artifacts/playtest/matches/<date>_bf1_yav2.jsonl`.
3. `python3 scripts/human_profile.py show <logs>`; then `python3 scripts/human_profile.py calibrate <logs> --seeds 8`.
4. Put the best point into `Strength::Good`, drop PROVISIONAL, re-run `--difficulty-check`.

Dry run (a skill 17 / reaction 9 proxy's two rounds as the "human", grid skill 14/17/20 x reaction 6/9, 3 seeds):
the loop runs end to end, but picked skill 20 / reaction 9 (distance 0.073 vs 0.167 for the true point): round to
round noise (K/D 0.9-1.8 for the same profile) is larger than the profile's effect below skill 20. Use 8 seeds and
all the matches; treat a fit inside 20 % on kills/min and accuracy as the bar, K/D as a check.

## Open
